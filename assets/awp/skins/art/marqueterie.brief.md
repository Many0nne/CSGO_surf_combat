# Prompt (to paste into the image model, with `gabarit_masque.png` attached as the input image)

Design a decorative WOOD MARQUETRY panel that exactly fills the black shape of the attached template image. The black
shape is an irregular flat panel: a long narrow horizontal band on the left that widens into a larger section on the
right, with two white openings (a small rectangular one in the middle, a rounded octagonal one further right). Keep the
exact same canvas size, shape position and outline. Everything outside the black shape and inside the two openings stays
plain white. Flat 2D artwork, seen straight on: no perspective, no 3D object, no shading of the panel, no reflections,
even flat lighting. No text, no logo, no signature.

STYLE: a real Art Nouveau marquetry panel, as made by the Nancy school cabinetmakers around 1900 (Emile Gallé, Louis
Majorelle): a picture made of thin wood veneers cut and inlaid edge to edge. Photographed flat, as a straight-on scan of
the real object.
- Every shape is a flat piece of veneer with a crisp cut edge and a hairline dark seam around it. Shapes are simple and
  bold, readable at a small size. Inner detail comes ONLY from the natural grain and figure of each wood, plus a few
  engraved lines (feather tips, the eye), never from painted shading.
- Lighting and colour come only from the woods: no glow, no rim light, no neon, no gradients, no airbrush, no sparkles,
  no fantasy colours. Satin varnished finish.
- Restraint: at least half of the surface is plain dark wenge background with nothing on it. One main subject, a few
  secondary elements, lots of calm empty space.

PALETTE (only these materials):
- background: wenge wood stained almost black (very dark brown, fine straight grain);
- pale holly / maple (cream white): the heron;
- pearwood or cherry (warm pink-brown): the heron's beak and legs, a few buds;
- walnut (mid brown) and olive-dyed sycamore (muted sage green): stems, reeds, leaves;
- mother-of-pearl (white with a soft pink-green sheen, only on the moon).

COMPOSITION (see `gabarit_annote.png`, orange box = focal point):
1. MAIN SUBJECT, inside the orange box (where the narrow band meets the wider section, just above the small
   rectangular opening): a grey heron standing in profile, facing LEFT, neck in a calm S curve, long beak, wings
   folded. The WHOLE bird fits inside the box and the black shape: not cut by the edges, not crossing the openings. A
   few tall reeds behind and in front of it, rising from the bottom edge of the shape.
2. The long narrow band on the left: two or three long flowing stems of wild umbels (Queen Anne's lace) and a few
   slender leaves, running horizontally from the heron towards the left end, in the Gallé manner. Sparse, elegant,
   lots of dark background between them.
3. The wide section on the right (around and beyond the octagonal opening): a mother-of-pearl moon (full disc, plain,
   about a third of the section height) in the upper area, fully inside the shape and not touching the opening; a few
   reeds rising from the bottom edge; nothing else.
4. Keep a thin band along the whole outline of the shape (about 1 % of the image height) free of important detail: a
   plain border is added there afterwards.

AVOID: neon, glow, rainbow colours, glossy fantasy art, digital painting look, over-detailed busy surface, symmetry or
repeating patterns, anything cut by the openings or the edges, text, photorealistic 3D, blur, noise.

OUTPUT: PNG, same aspect ratio and size as the input (2520 x 1080, or 1536 x 1024 with the `3x2` template), as sharp as
possible. Several variants welcome.
