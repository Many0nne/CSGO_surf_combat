# Prompt (to paste into the image model, with `gabarit_masque.png` attached as the input image)

Paint a weapon-skin illustration INSIDE the black silhouette of the attached image, which is the exact side profile of a
sniper rifle stock (handguard on the left, stock on the right, two white holes: trigger guard and thumbhole). Keep the
exact same canvas size, silhouette position and shape. Everything outside the silhouette and inside the two holes stays
plain white. Do not draw a gun, a barrel, a scope or any 3D object: this is a flat 2D artwork that will be wrapped onto the
stock, so no perspective, no 3D shading of the stock, no reflections, even flat lighting. No text, no logo, no watermark,
no signature.

STYLE: clean, detailed, stylized illustration like premium Counter-Strike weapon skins (AWP Hyper Beast, AWP Neo-Noir,
AWP Medusa): crisp confident linework, graphic cel-shading, rich detail in the main subject, high contrast, saturated
colours, readable at small size. Not a photo, not a blurry painting, not a cartoon for kids.

THEME: "Night hunt" on a black-light velvet poster. Background: deep violet-black velvet night sky, subtle soft velvet
texture. Accents in glowing fluorescent cyan, hot magenta and lime green, like a 70s black-light poster: thin glowing
outlines, rim lights and motion streaks.

COMPOSITION (left = towards the muzzle, right = butt):
1. MAIN SUBJECT, the front part of the stock just right of the handguard/stock joint and above the trigger guard hole
   (orange box in `gabarit_annote.png`): a great horned owl diving towards the left, talons stretched forward, wings
   swept back tight against its body, head turned to look straight at the viewer with big glowing amber eyes and ear
   tufts. The WHOLE owl fits inside the silhouette: not cut by the edges, not crossing the holes. Detailed feathers with
   fluorescent rim lights.
2. Handguard (the long bar on the left): flowing speed and wind streaks trailing from the owl towards the muzzle, in
   fluorescent cyan / magenta / lime, a few curling swirls, a few small stars. Lighter detail than the owl.
3. Rear stock (right part, around and behind the thumbhole): a large full moon fully inside the silhouette (not cut by
   the thumbhole or the edges), a pine forest silhouette rising from the bottom edge, a few falling feathers.
4. Keep a thin band along the whole silhouette outline (about 1 % of the image height) free of important detail: the
   game paints its own border there.

AVOID: repeating or symmetrical patterns, anything cut by the holes, text, a visible gun, photorealism, blur, noise.

OUTPUT: PNG, same aspect ratio and size as the input (2520 x 1080, or 1536 x 1024 with the `3x2` template), as sharp as
possible. Several variants welcome.
