# Prompt (to paste into the image model, with `gabarit_rig.png` attached as the input image)

Dress the three blocky characters of the attached image in the same outfit: a DESERT FIGHTER. The attached image is a
turnaround of one character built from simple rounded blocks (a toy-like video game avatar): FRONT view on the left,
THREE-QUARTER front view in the middle, BACK view on the right. Keep the exact same canvas size, the three characters at
the same positions, the same scale and the same pose. Plain white background, soft even studio lighting, no cast
shadows on the background, no ground, no scenery.

BODY RULES (very important):
- The body stays made of the same blocks with the same proportions: square torso, square arms and legs, rounded head.
  No realistic human anatomy, no visible muscles, no fingers, no neck. The outfit is painted onto the faces of the
  blocks like a texture, plus a few added volumes listed below. The added volumes stick out a little from the blocks,
  never more than about a quarter of a block width.
- Clean, stylized look of a well-made blocky game character: big readable shapes, 3 or 4 main colours, clear contrast
  between them, matte materials, fine fabric texture only (weave, stitching), no clutter. It must still read on a
  character seen small, from 30 metres away.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no flag, no badge or insignia of a real army. No weapon.

OUTFIT: a fighter dressed for sand and wind, everything wrapped and tied.
- HEAD: a large check-patterned scarf wrapped around the whole head in thick folds, covering the top of the head, the
  mouth and the nose, leaving only a band for the eyes. Over this band, a pair of old motorcycle goggles with round
  amber-tinted lenses and a leather strap. One loose end of the scarf hangs down over one shoulder at the back. No face
  visible except a little skin around the goggles.
- TORSO: a long-sleeved sand-coloured shirt; over it, a simple chest rig (a wide horizontal band of four rectangular
  pouches across the chest, held by straps over the shoulders and crossing in an X on the back), and two cartridge
  belts crossing in an X over the chest on top of it (the signature shape of the outfit). A plain wide belt at the
  waist.
- ARMS: sleeves rolled up to the elbow on one arm only, cloth wraps around the forearms and the hands (plain, no fingers
  drawn).
- LEGS: loose khaki trousers, a thigh pouch on one side, the bottoms tucked into worn dark brown leather boots.

PALETTE (only these):
- sand beige and pale khaki: shirt and trousers;
- brick red and off-white: the check pattern of the scarf (simple, large checks);
- coyote brown: chest rig, pouches, belts;
- dark brown leather: boots, goggle strap; brass: cartridges of the belts, goggle rims;
- warm amber: goggle lenses.

AVOID: realistic human body, sci-fi elements, glowing parts, neon, gradients, airbrush, a turban or a robe that would
read as a religious or traditional costume (it must read as practical combat gear), text or markings, weapons,
background scenery, photorealism.

OUTPUT: PNG, 1536 x 1024, same layout as the input, as sharp as possible. Several variants welcome.
