# Prompt (to paste into the image model, with `gabarit_rig.png` attached as the input image)

Dress the three blocky characters of the attached image in the same outfit: a CHEMICAL PROTECTION SUIT. The attached
image is a turnaround of one character built from simple rounded blocks (a toy-like video game avatar): FRONT view on
the left, THREE-QUARTER front view in the middle, BACK view on the right. Keep the exact same canvas size, the three
characters at the same positions, the same scale and the same pose. Plain white background, soft even studio lighting,
no cast shadows on the background, no ground, no scenery.

BODY RULES (very important):
- The body stays made of the same blocks with the same proportions: square torso, square arms and legs, rounded head.
  No realistic human anatomy, no visible muscles, no fingers, no neck. The outfit is painted onto the faces of the
  blocks like a texture, plus a few added volumes listed below. The added volumes stick out a little from the blocks,
  never more than about a quarter of a block width.
- Clean, stylized look of a well-made blocky game character: big readable shapes, 3 or 4 main colours, clear contrast
  between them, no clutter. It must still read on a character seen small, from 30 metres away.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no hazard symbol, no flag, no badge. No weapon.

OUTFIT: a one-piece rubberised chemical protection suit, with a gas mask.
- HEAD: the hood of the suit covers the whole head and is drawn tight around a black rubber gas mask that covers the
  face: one large single visor (two round lenses joined in one frame, or a single wide panoramic lens) and two round
  filter canisters, one on each side of the mouth, angled slightly down and back. No face visible.
- SUIT: semi-glossy rubberised fabric with a few stiff folds and creases (at the waist, the elbows and the knees) and
  sealed seams drawn as slightly raised lines. A vertical zip under a covering flap down the middle of the chest. On the
  back, a flat rectangular pack (small air tank in a harness), sticking out only a little.
- HANDS AND FEET: thick black rubber gloves (plain mittens, no fingers drawn) and black rubber boots; at each wrist and
  each ankle, two or three turns of grey duct tape sealing the glove or the boot to the suit (the signature detail).

PALETTE (only these):
- deep mustard / chrome yellow (warm, not fluorescent): the suit;
- black rubber: mask, filters, gloves, boots, back pack;
- dull silver grey: duct tape and the metal rims of the filters;
- dark green-grey glass with a soft reflection: the mask visor.

AVOID: realistic human body, sci-fi armour, glowing parts, neon or fluorescent colours, gradients, airbrush, a big
backpack, hazard stripes or symbols, text or markings, weapons, background scenery, photorealism.

OUTPUT: PNG, 1536 x 1024, same layout as the input, as sharp as possible. Several variants welcome.
