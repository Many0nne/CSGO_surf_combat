# Prompt (to paste into the image model, with `gabarit_rig.png` attached as the input image)

Dress the three blocky characters of the attached image in the same outfit: a BOMB DISPOSAL SUIT. The attached image is a
turnaround of one character built from simple rounded blocks (a toy-like video game avatar): FRONT view on the left,
THREE-QUARTER front view in the middle, BACK view on the right. Keep the exact same canvas size, the three characters at
the same positions, the same scale and the same pose. Plain white background, soft even studio lighting, no cast
shadows on the background, no ground, no scenery.

BODY RULES (very important):
- The body stays made of the same blocks with the same proportions: square torso, square arms and legs, rounded head.
  No realistic human anatomy, no visible muscles, no fingers, no neck. The outfit is painted onto the faces of the
  blocks like a texture, plus a few added volumes listed below. The added volumes stick out a little from the blocks,
  never more than about a quarter of a block width, except where stated.
- Clean, stylized look of a well-made blocky game character: big readable shapes, 3 or 4 main colours, clear contrast
  between them, matte materials, fine fabric texture only (stitching, quilting), no clutter. It must still read on a
  character seen small, from 30 metres away.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no flag, no badge or insignia of a real army or police force. No weapon.

OUTFIT: a heavy bomb disposal suit, the most massive and protected character of the series.
- HEAD: a rounded bomb disposal helmet that covers the whole head, only slightly larger than the head (about 15 %
  wider), with a wide dark smoked visor across the front and a thick padded rim around the visor. No face visible.
- NECK AND SHOULDERS: a thick padded collar that rises from the top of the torso up to the bottom of the helmet, all the
  way round. Large padded shoulder pieces on top of the arms (they may stick out up to half a block width upwards and
  outwards: this is the signature volume of the outfit).
- TORSO FRONT: one large rigid armour plate covering most of the chest and belly, with a thick padded border; below it,
  a smaller armoured flap hangs over the hips. The back of the torso is quilted fabric with two short wide straps, no
  plate.
- ARMS AND LEGS: thick padded sleeves and trousers in quilted panels (horizontal quilting lines), a separate rigid knee
  plate and a forearm plate on each limb. Heavy black gloves (plain mittens, no fingers drawn) and heavy black boots.

PALETTE (only these):
- olive drab green: the quilted suit fabric;
- black / very dark charcoal: armour plates, collar rim, gloves, boots;
- smoked dark grey with a soft reflection: the visor;
- muted sand yellow: only thin trims (plate borders, strap buckles), small touches.

AVOID: realistic human body, sci-fi or power armour, glowing parts, neon, gradients, airbrush, extra pouches everywhere,
a giant oversized helmet, text or markings, weapons, background scenery, photorealism.

OUTPUT: PNG, 1536 x 1024, same layout as the input, as sharp as possible. Several variants welcome.
