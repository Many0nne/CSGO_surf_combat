# Prompt (to paste into the image model, with `gabarit_rig.png` attached as the input image)

Dress the three blocky characters of the attached image in the same outfit: RIOT ARMOUR. The attached image is a
turnaround of one character built from simple rounded blocks (a toy-like video game avatar): FRONT view on the left,
THREE-QUARTER front view in the middle, BACK view on the right. Keep the exact same canvas size, the three characters at
the same positions, the same scale and the same pose. Plain white background, soft even studio lighting, no cast
shadows on the background, no ground, no scenery.

BODY RULES (very important):
- The body stays made of the same blocks with the same proportions: square torso, square arms and legs, rounded head.
  No realistic human anatomy, no visible muscles, no fingers, no neck. The outfit is painted onto the faces of the
  blocks like a texture, plus a few added volumes listed below. The added volumes stick out a little from the blocks,
  never more than about a quarter of a block width, except where stated.
- Clean, stylized look of a well-made blocky game character: big readable shapes, 3 or 4 main colours, clear contrast
  between them, matte materials, no clutter. It must still read on a character seen small, from 30 metres away.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no "POLICE" or any word, no logo, no flag, no badge or insignia of a real force. No
  weapon, no shield, no baton.

OUTFIT: crowd-control armour made of hard segmented shells over dark fabric, like a beetle.
- HEAD: a round helmet covering the whole head, with a clear transparent face visor pulled down; behind the visor, a
  plain simple face (mid skin tone, two small dark oval eyes, no other features). A short rigid neck guard hangs from
  the back of the helmet.
- SHOULDERS: large shoulder guards made of three overlapping curved plates stacked like roof tiles, covering the top
  of each arm (they may stick out up to half a block width upwards and outwards: this is the signature volume of the
  outfit).
- TORSO: a rigid chest and back shell with a horizontal segmented belly section below the chest (three or four
  overlapping bands), over a dark navy fabric suit.
- ARMS: segmented forearm guards (two or three overlapping bands) on the lower arms, plain dark gloves (no fingers
  drawn).
- LEGS: a thigh guard on each upper leg, and a large knee-and-shin guard on each lower leg (one rounded knee cup on top
  of two or three overlapping bands), heavy black boots.
- A thin light grey reflective strip along the edge of every plate, so the segments read clearly.

PALETTE (only these):
- matte charcoal black: all hard shells and the helmet;
- very dark navy blue: the fabric suit between the shells;
- light grey: the reflective edge strips;
- clear glass with a soft reflection: the visor.

AVOID: realistic human body, sci-fi or futuristic power armour, glowing parts, neon, gradients, airbrush, text, words or
markings of any kind, shields and weapons, background scenery, photorealism.

OUTPUT: PNG, 1536 x 1024, same layout as the input, as sharp as possible. Several variants welcome.
