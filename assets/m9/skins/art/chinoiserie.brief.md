# Prompt (to paste into the image model, with `gabarit_masque.png` attached as the input image)

Paint a weapon-skin illustration INSIDE the black silhouette of the attached image, which is the exact side profile of a
combat knife blade (tip on the left, sharp cutting edge along the top, saw teeth along the bottom, guard on the right,
one white oval hole). Keep the exact same canvas size, silhouette position and shape. Everything outside the silhouette,
inside the oval hole and between the saw teeth stays plain white. Do not draw a knife, a handle or any 3D object: this
is a flat 2D artwork that will be printed onto the blade, so no perspective, no 3D shading of the blade, no metal
reflections, even flat lighting. No text, no Chinese characters, no red seal stamps, no logo, no watermark, no signature.

STYLE: clean, detailed, stylized illustration like premium Counter-Strike weapon skins (Hyper Beast, Neo-Noir, Medusa),
in the manner of an 18th-century hand-painted chinoiserie silk wallpaper: crisp confident brush linework, graphic flat
colour areas, fine gold-leaf outlines and highlights, rich detail in the main subject, high contrast, readable at small
size. Not a photo, not a blurry watercolour, not a cartoon for kids.

THEME: "Jade wallpaper". Background: luminous jade-green painted silk, with a subtle silk sheen and a few soft bands of
pale gold mist. Accents: real gold leaf, blossom pink, ivory white, ink black.

COMPOSITION (left = towards the tip, right = towards the guard):
1. MAIN SUBJECT, in the right part of the blade between the oval hole and the guard (orange box in
   `gabarit_annote.png`): a white heron in flight towards the tip, wings spread wide and long along the blade, long neck
   folded in an S, black crest feather, yellow eye looking at the viewer, legs trailing back towards the guard. The WHOLE
   heron fits inside the silhouette: not cut by the edges, below the top steel band, above the saw teeth. Detailed
   feathers outlined in gold.
2. Towards the tip (left part, around the hole): one flowering cherry branch sweeping from the hole towards the tip, with
   pink and ivory blossoms and a few drifting petals; the branch goes around the hole, nothing important is cut by it.
3. Near the guard (right end): a few gold-outlined rocks and bamboo leaves rising from the bottom edge, lighter detail.
4. Keep the top band of the blade (about the top 12 % of the blade height, hatched in blue in `gabarit_annote.png`) free
   of anything important: it becomes the bare steel cutting edge. Keep a thin band along the whole outline (about 1 %
   of the image height) free of important detail: the game paints its own border there.

AVOID: repeating or symmetrical patterns, anything cut by the hole or the teeth, text or characters, seals, a visible
knife, photorealism, blur, noise, a red dominant colour.

OUTPUT: PNG, same aspect ratio and size as the input (2520 x 1080, or 1536 x 1024 with the `3x2` template), as sharp as
possible. Several variants welcome.
