# Prompt (to paste into the image model, with `gabarit_masque.png` attached as the input image)

Paint a weapon-skin illustration INSIDE the black silhouette of the attached image, which is the exact side profile of a
combat knife blade (tip on the left, sharp cutting edge along the top, saw teeth along the bottom, guard on the right,
one white oval hole). Keep the exact same canvas size, silhouette position and shape. Everything outside the silhouette,
inside the oval hole and between the saw teeth stays plain white. Do not draw a knife, a handle or any 3D object: this
is a flat 2D artwork that will be printed onto the blade, so no perspective, no 3D shading of the blade, no metal
reflections, even flat lighting. No text, no banner, no coat of arms, no logo, no watermark, no signature.

STYLE: clean, detailed, stylized illustration like premium Counter-Strike weapon skins (Hyper Beast, Neo-Noir, Medusa),
in the manner of a late-medieval "millefleurs" tapestry (The Hunt of the Unicorn, The Lady and the Unicorn): flat
graphic shapes with crisp outlines, a limited palette of dyed wool colours, and a visible woven texture (fine horizontal
weft threads) over the whole artwork. Rich detail in the main subject, high contrast, readable at small size. Not a
photo, not a blurry painting, not a cartoon for kids.

THEME: "Millefleurs hunt". Background: very deep midnight-blue wool ground with dark bottle-green foliage, densely and
irregularly strewn with small flowering plants (violets, daisies, wild strawberries, carnations, bluebells), each one
drawn individually. Accents: ivory white, madder red, saffron yellow, a little gold thread.

COMPOSITION (left = towards the tip, right = towards the guard):
1. MAIN SUBJECT, in the right part of the blade between the oval hole and the guard (orange box in
   `gabarit_annote.png`): a white stag leaping towards the tip, body stretched long along the blade, forelegs tucked,
   head raised and turned back towards the viewer, antlers fully drawn, a thin gold collar. The WHOLE stag, antlers
   included, fits inside the silhouette: not cut by the edges, below the top steel band, above the saw teeth.
2. Towards the tip (left part, around the hole): the flower field continues, with a small white rabbit and a small
   songbird among the flowers, smaller and less detailed than the stag; nothing important is cut by the hole.
3. Near the guard (right end): a few taller flowering plants and a small oak sapling with acorns rising from the bottom
   edge, lighter detail.
4. Keep the top band of the blade (about the top 12 % of the blade height, hatched in blue in `gabarit_annote.png`) free
   of anything important: it becomes the bare steel cutting edge. Keep a thin band along the whole outline (about 1 %
   of the image height) free of important detail: the game paints its own border there.

AVOID: a regular repeating flower pattern, symmetry, anything cut by the hole or the teeth, text, a visible knife,
photorealism, blur, noise, a bright cobalt or white-and-blue tile look, a green dominant colour.

OUTPUT: PNG, same aspect ratio and size as the input (2520 x 1080, or 1536 x 1024 with the `3x2` template), as sharp as
possible. Several variants welcome.
