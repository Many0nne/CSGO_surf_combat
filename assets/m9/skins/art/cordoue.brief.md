# Prompt (to paste into the image model, with `gabarit_masque.png` attached as the input image)

Design a decorative GILT LEATHER panel that exactly fills the black shape of the attached template image. The black
shape is a long, flat horizontal panel: pointed at the left end, cut square at the right end. Its top edge is straight
on the right half and slopes down to the point on the left. Its bottom edge has a row of nine small rounded notches on
the right half. There is one white oval opening in the left-middle part. Keep the exact same canvas size, shape position
and outline. Everything outside the black shape, inside the oval opening and inside the notches stays plain white. Flat
2D artwork, seen straight on: no perspective, no 3D object, no shading of the panel, no reflections, even flat
lighting. No text, no logo, no signature.

STYLE: a real Spanish "guadamecí" (Cordoba gilt leather wall hanging, 17th century), photographed flat, as a straight-on
scan of the real object.
- The ground is burnished gold leaf on leather, warm and even, covered with a fine, regular punched texture of tiny dots
  made with a metal punch (a calm, even stipple, not noise).
- Motifs are outlined with a crisp dark brown line, as if tooled into the leather, and coloured with flat transparent
  glazes laid over the gold, so the gold glows through the colour. Shapes are simple and bold, readable at a small size.
  Inner detail comes only from a few tooled lines (feather barbs, seeds, leaf veins), never from painted shading.
- Lighting and colour come only from the gold and the glazes: no glow, no rim light, no neon, no gradients, no
  airbrush, no sparkles, no shiny metal glare, no cast shadows, no embossed 3D look.
- Restraint: this is ONE composition, not a repeating wallpaper pattern. At least 40 % of the surface is plain stippled
  gold with nothing on it. One main subject, a few secondary elements, calm empty space.

PALETTE (only these colours):
- background: burnished gold leaf with the punched dot texture;
- red lake glaze (deep transparent crimson): pomegranates, a few flowers;
- copper-green glaze (verdigris): leaves, scrolls, the peacock's body and neck;
- a little deep blue glaze, only on the peacock's neck and the eyes of its tail;
- dark brown: all outlines and tooled lines.

COMPOSITION (left = towards the point, right = towards the square end):
1. MAIN SUBJECT, in the right part of the shape, between the oval opening and the square right end, above the notches:
   a peacock walking in profile, facing LEFT, head raised, small crown of feathers, its long closed tail train flowing
   horizontally behind it towards the right end, with a row of large tail eyes (gold, red lake, blue, green rings). The
   WHOLE bird and tail fit inside the shape: not cut by the edges, not crossing the notches or the opening. A faint
   horizontal line runs at mid-height of the right part: keep the peacock's head and eye off it (place the head above
   it).
2. The left part (around the oval opening, towards the point): one acanthus scroll that starts behind the peacock and
   curls towards the point, bearing two or three split pomegranates (red lake skin, rows of seeds) and a few leaves. The
   scroll goes around the opening; nothing important is cut by it.
3. Near the square right end: a small tuft of leaves and one pomegranate flower rising from the bottom edge, lighter
   detail.
4. Keep the top band of the shape (about the top 12 % of its height, along the whole top edge) plain stippled gold with
   no motif: it is polished away afterwards. Keep a thin band along the whole outline (about 1 % of the image height)
   free of important detail: a plain border is added there afterwards.

AVOID: repeating or symmetrical patterns, a coat of arms, heraldry, human or angel figures, faces, seams between leather
panels, a decorative frame or border, neon, glow, rainbow colours, glossy fantasy art, digital painting look,
over-detailed busy surface, anything cut by the opening, the notches or the edges, text, photorealistic 3D, blur, noise.

OUTPUT: PNG, same aspect ratio and size as the input (2520 x 1080, or 1536 x 1024 with the `3x2` template), as sharp as
possible. Several variants welcome.
